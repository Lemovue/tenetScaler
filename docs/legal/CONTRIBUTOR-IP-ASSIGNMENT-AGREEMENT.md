### A2. Why a documentation step is needed

Commit authorship in git does not by itself establish who owns the copyright. Code may have moved between branches, been merged or squashed by someone other than its author, been written together, or been produced with the help of tools. Each Founder's original contributions therefore have to be identified and confirmed in writing.

The four git author identities in the history of Lemovue/tenetScaler correspond to the four Founders' GitHub accounts. This is to be confirmed in writing when the Founders IP Ownership Agreement is signed.

### A3. Process

1. **Starting map.** [`LICENSING-AUDIT.md`](LICENSING-AUDIT.md) contains a map of commits, pull requests and surviving lines per git author and per area of the code. It is a starting point only. Surviving-line counts measure who committed code, not who wrote it, and are not ownership shares.
2. **Founder review.** Each Founder reviews the map and completes their part of Schedule 3 ("Founder Contribution Record") of the Founders IP Ownership Agreement. The record lists the areas, branches, pull requests, documents and other assets the Founder created, and marks:
   - any part copied or adapted from third-party material;
   - any part not created by the Founder personally, naming its source;
   - any part created jointly with another person, naming them;
   - any employer, university, sponsor or other third-party claim (Schedule 4 of that agreement).
3. **Cross-check.** The four Founders check the four records against each other and against the starting map. Any area that no Founder claims, or that more than one Founder claims, is resolved in writing before signature.
4. **Assignment.** Each Founder signs the Founders IP Ownership Agreement. Its assignment clause brings that Founder's contributions into the collective ownership in the agreed Ownership Shares. Until all four have signed, no transfer has taken place.
5. **Other authors.** The git history shows no author other than the four Founders. If anyone else is found to have contributed material (for example to design, documents or assets outside git), that person either:
   - signs Part B, listing their existing contributions in Schedule A; or
   - is dealt with by another route the Founders approve unanimously in writing (for example, removing and rewriting that person's contributions, or admitting them as a co-owner by written amendment of the Founders IP Ownership Agreement).
6. **Record.** The Founders record the date each step was completed in a private record kept with the executed agreement, and update section 10 of `LICENSING-AUDIT.md`.
